EXPENSE TOOL - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Document ID: FIN-TOOL-001
Version: 2.1
Effective date: 20 January 2026
Owner: Finance Operations
Classification: Internal


This document explains how to operate the expense tool. It does not set policy.
What may be claimed, and within what limits, is determined by the business
travel and expense reimbursement policy and by the corporate credit card rules.
If this FAQ appears to contradict either of those, they win.


HOW DO I GET INTO THE TOOL?

Through single sign-on, from the "Expenses" tile on the intranet home page.
There is no separate password. If the tile is missing, your account has not
been enrolled yet -- raise a P4 ticket with the IT service desk.


WHAT IS A REPORT AND WHY DO I NEED ONE?

Individual expense lines are grouped into a report, and reports are what
managers approve. A report should cover one trip or one calendar month of
ad-hoc costs. Mixing several trips into one report makes approval slower
because the approver has to reconstruct which cost belongs to which purpose.


HOW DO I ADD A RECEIPT?

Three ways:

  - Photograph it in the mobile app. Optical character recognition fills in the
    date, merchant and amount, which you then check.
  - Forward the merchant's e-mail receipt to receipts@northwind.example. It
    arrives in your inbox inside the tool within a few minutes.
  - Upload a PDF or image directly to the expense line in the web interface.

Accepted formats are PDF, JPG, PNG and HEIC. The maximum file size is 10 MB per
attachment.


THE CARD TRANSACTION IS THERE BUT I HAVE NO RECEIPT YET. WHAT NOW?

Leave the transaction unsubmitted and attach the receipt when you have it. Do
not submit the line with a note promising the receipt later -- Finance will
reject it and the line returns to your inbox.


WHY DOES MY REPORT SAY "MISSING BUSINESS PURPOSE"?

Every line needs a purpose in plain language: who, what and why. "Client
lunch" is not enough. "Lunch with A. Weber and M. Frank, Contoso, discussing
the Q2 renewal" is.

For hospitality, the names and employers of everyone present are required.


HOW DO I SPLIT ONE TRANSACTION ACROSS TWO COST CENTRES?

Open the line, choose "Itemise", and add a row per cost centre with its amount.
The rows must sum to the transaction total or the line will not submit.


WHAT CURRENCY SHOULD I ENTER?

Enter the amount exactly as it appears on the receipt, in the receipt's own
currency. The tool converts using the card issuer's settlement rate for card
transactions, or the European Central Bank reference rate on the transaction
date for cash. Do not convert manually; a manually converted amount will not
reconcile.


WHO APPROVES MY REPORT?

Your line manager by default. If the report contains a line above the delegated
limit for your grade, the tool adds the department head as a second approver
automatically. You do not need to request this.


HOW LONG DOES REIMBURSEMENT TAKE?

Approved out-of-pocket claims are paid in the next payment run. Runs are
weekly, on Wednesdays, with a cut-off of Monday 12:00 CET. Card transactions
are settled directly with the issuer and are not paid to you.


MY REPORT WAS REJECTED. WHAT HAPPENS?

It returns to your inbox with the approver's comment. Fix the issue and
resubmit; the approval history is preserved. Rejection is not a disciplinary
step.


I LEFT A REPORT UNSUBMITTED FOR A LONG TIME. IS THAT A PROBLEM?

Yes. The tool sends reminders at 14, 30 and 45 days after the transaction date.
Deadlines and the consequences of missing them are set by the travel and
expense policy and the corporate card rules, not by this tool.


CAN I DELEGATE MY EXPENSES TO AN ASSISTANT?

Yes. Under Settings, "Delegates", you can grant another employee permission to
prepare and submit reports on your behalf. Approval rights cannot be delegated
this way; approval delegation is configured by Finance Operations.


WHO DO I ASK IF SOMETHING IS BROKEN?

Tool faults go to the IT service desk. Questions about whether something is
claimable go to Finance Operations.
