NORTHWIND SYSTEMS GMBH
FLEXIBLE AND DISTRIBUTED WORKING POLICY

Document ID: HR-FLX-007
Version: 2.4
Effective date: 1 March 2026
Owner: People Operations
Classification: Internal
Review cycle: Annual


1. PURPOSE AND PRINCIPLE

Northwind Systems operates a hybrid working model. The principle is that
focused individual work is done wherever the employee is most effective, while
collaboration, mentoring and team decision-making benefit from shared physical
presence.

This policy sets out the baseline expectation, the exception process, and the
obligations that apply wherever an employee works from.


2. BASELINE EXPECTATION

Hybrid is the default arrangement for all office-based roles.

Employees are expected to be present at their assigned site for a minimum of
two days per week. The specific days are agreed within the team, not assigned
centrally, and teams are encouraged to align on shared anchor days so that the
office days actually overlap.

The two-day minimum is measured as a weekly average over a calendar month, so
that travel, training and leave in a given week do not create a breach.

Roles that are inherently site-bound -- facilities, reception, hardware
logistics, laboratory work -- are excluded from the hybrid model and are
specified in the employee's contract.


3. FULLY DISTRIBUTED ARRANGEMENTS

An arrangement in which an employee does not attend a Northwind site at all is
treated as an exception to this policy. It is not available on request as a
standing entitlement.

An exception requires:

  - A written case from the employee describing the arrangement and its
    duration.
  - Support from the line manager, including confirmation that the role can be
    performed with no on-site presence and that the team's collaboration
    pattern accommodates it.
  - Approval by the department head. This approval cannot be delegated.
  - A review date, at most twelve months out, at which the arrangement is
    either renewed or ended.

Approved exceptions are recorded by People Operations. An arrangement agreed
informally within a team, without department head approval, has no standing and
will be corrected at the next review.


4. WORKING FROM ANOTHER COUNTRY

Working from a country other than the one in the employment contract creates
tax, social security and permanent establishment obligations for the company.
It therefore requires approval from both the department head and Finance,
regardless of duration.

Short stays of up to twenty working days per calendar year within the European
Union may be approved by the department head alone, with notification to
Finance. Anything longer, or anywhere outside the European Union, requires the
full approval path and a minimum of six weeks' notice.


5. OBLIGATIONS THAT APPLY OFF-SITE

The location of work does not change any other obligation. In particular:

  - Core availability. Employees are reachable during the team's agreed core
    hours and keep their calendar accurate.
  - Equipment. Company work is performed on company-managed devices only.
    Personal machines are not permitted for company work, whatever the
    location.
  - Network. Public wireless networks may be used only with the corporate VPN
    active.
  - Confidentiality. Screens must not be visible to people outside the
    company, and calls covering confidential material must not be taken in
    public spaces.
  - Storage. The information security policy applies unchanged. Company data
    stays in approved corporate storage.

6. HOME WORKSTATION

Employees working off-site regularly may request a second docking station,
display and input devices through the IT service desk equipment request
process. Furniture is not provided. An annual home workstation allowance of
EUR 250 is available and is claimed through the expense tool under the
category "home office equipment".


7. OFFICE SPACE

Desks at all sites are unassigned and booked through the desk booking tool.
Bookings open fourteen days ahead. A booking that is not checked in by 11:00 is
released automatically.
