NORTHWIND SYSTEMS GMBH
FACILITIES AND OFFICE GUIDE

Document ID: FAC-GEN-001
Version: 4.0
Effective date: 8 January 2026
Owner: Facilities Management
Classification: Internal


1. SITES

  Munich (headquarters)   Leonrodstrasse 118, four floors, approximately 260
                          desks, main reception on the ground floor.
  Hamburg                 Grosse Elbstrasse 42, one floor, approximately 60
                          desks, reception shared with the building.
  Krakow                  ul. Pawia 9, two floors, approximately 90 desks.

Reception at every site is staffed from 08:30 to 17:30 on business days.


2. BUILDING ACCESS

Entry is by access badge at all sites. The badge also operates the internal
floor doors and the bicycle store.

Standard access hours are 06:00 to 22:00, Monday to Sunday. Outside these
hours the badge readers are disabled and entry requires prior arrangement with
Facilities Management, made at least one business day in advance.

Badges are issued by the IT service desk together with the notebook during
onboarding. Access rights are set by the line manager's request and reviewed
quarterly. The procedure for a lost badge is set out in the information
security policy.

Do not hold doors open for people you do not recognise, including people
carrying deliveries. Anyone without a badge is a visitor and must be signed in
at reception.


3. VISITORS

Visitors are registered in advance through the visitor booking form on the
intranet, with name, employer and expected arrival time. Reception issues a
visitor pass on arrival against photo identification.

Visitors are accompanied at all times by their host and may not be left alone
in working areas. The host returns the pass to reception on departure.

Visitor parking is available at the front entrance of the Munich and Krakow
sites. Spaces are requested through reception when the visit is registered.


4. MEETING ROOMS

Rooms are booked in the calendar system. Room names are shown on the door
panel, which also allows an ad-hoc booking if the room is free.

  - A booking not checked in on the door panel within ten minutes is released.
  - Rooms seating twelve or more may not be booked as a recurring series
    without Facilities Management approval.
  - Two rooms per site are designated quiet rooms for confidential calls and
    cannot be booked in advance.

Leave rooms as you found them: whiteboards cleared, chairs replaced, no cups.


5. DESKS

All desks are unassigned and are booked through the desk booking tool, which
opens fourteen days ahead. A booking not checked in by 11:00 is released.

Personal belongings are not left on desks overnight. Lockers are available on
each floor and are allocated through Facilities Management.


6. KITCHENS AND CATERING

Kitchens on each floor provide coffee, tea, filtered water and refrigeration.
The Munich site has a staffed canteen on the ground floor, open 11:30 to 14:00
on business days, subsidised at 40 percent.

Label anything you put in a refrigerator. Refrigerators are emptied every
Friday at 17:00 and unlabelled items are discarded.


7. BICYCLES AND SHOWERS

Covered bicycle storage is available at all three sites, accessed by badge.
Munich and Krakow have shower facilities on the ground floor.


8. DELIVERIES AND POST

Personal deliveries to any Northwind site are not accepted. Business
deliveries are received at the goods entrance and are notified to the
addressee by e-mail.


9. FAULTS AND REQUESTS

Report a facilities fault -- heating, lighting, plumbing, furniture, door
hardware -- through the facilities request form on the intranet. Faults
affecting safety or security are reported by telephone to extension 4100
immediately and are attended to the same day.

IT equipment faults go to the IT service desk, not to Facilities Management.


10. FIRE AND EVACUATION

Evacuation routes are posted on every floor. Assembly points are marked
outside each building. Drills are held twice a year. On hearing the alarm,
leave by the nearest marked route and do not use the lifts.

Floor wardens are identified by the yellow markings on their badge holders and
direct the evacuation on their floor.
