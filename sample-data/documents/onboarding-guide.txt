NEW JOINER ORIENTATION GUIDE

Effective date: 5 January 2026
Owner: People Operations

Review cycle: Annual


1. PURPOSE

This guide describes what an incoming employee should expect during their
initial weeks at Northwind Systems, and what the receiving team is expected to
prepare in advance. It applies to permanent staff, fixed-term staff and
long-term contractors joining any Northwind entity.

Line managers are responsible for completing the preparation steps in section 2
before the employee's start date. People Operations tracks completion and will
escalate any outstanding item to the department head.


2. PREPARATION BEFORE THE START DATE

The hiring manager must complete the following at least five working days in
advance:

  - Raise an equipment request so that a notebook, docking station and display
    are configured and ready for collection.
  - Request building access rights for the correct site and floor.
  - Nominate a buddy from within the team (see section 5).
  - Book the introductory sessions listed in section 6 into the calendar.
  - Confirm the seating allocation with the office coordinator.

People Operations issues the employment paperwork and payroll enrolment
separately. Nothing in this guide replaces those formalities.


3. ARRIVAL AND EQUIPMENT COLLECTION

Report to the reception desk of your assigned site between 09:00 and 10:00 on
your start date. Reception will notify your manager and issue a temporary
visitor pass for the morning.

The following must be completed before you begin any project work. People
Operations refers to this list as the arrival checklist, and it is tracked in
the onboarding portal:

  1. Collect your notebook and your permanent access badge from the IT service
     desk on the ground floor. Both are issued together and require photo
     identification.

  2. Complete the mandatory information security awareness training. The
     module takes roughly 45 minutes and is assigned to you automatically on
     your start date. Network access beyond the guest segment is not enabled
     until it is finished.

  3. Meet your assigned buddy. Your buddy will walk you through the office,
     the team's working agreements and the internal tooling landscape.

  4. Set up the accounts listed in the arrival checklist. The standard set is
     single sign-on, the source control platform, the ticketing system, the
     internal wiki and the expense tool. Additional accounts depend on the
     team and are requested by your manager.

Items 1 to 4 are normally finished before lunch. If any of them is blocked,
tell your manager rather than working around it -- an incomplete checklist is
the most common cause of access problems in the second week.


4. THE FIRST TWO WEEKS

Weeks one and two are deliberately light on delivery commitments. The
expectation is that you read the team documentation, attend the sessions in
section 6, and pick up one small, well-scoped piece of work to learn the
delivery pipeline end to end.

Your manager will agree a set of 30-day objectives with you by the end of week
one. These are orientation objectives, not performance targets; formal
performance review cycles are described in the performance review process
document and do not apply until you have completed a full cycle.


5. BUDDY AND MANAGER SUPPORT

Every incoming employee is paired with a designated buddy for their initial 30
days. The buddy is an experienced colleague from the same team, but never the
person's line manager. The buddy is the default first point of contact for
practical, day-to-day questions: how the team works, who owns what, which
channel to ask in, where a document lives.

The buddy commitment is:

  - A short check-in every day during the first week.
  - A weekly check-in during weeks two, three and four.
  - Availability for ad-hoc questions throughout the 30-day period.

In parallel, the line manager holds formal check-ins. These are scheduled at
the end of week one, the end of week four and the end of week twelve, and they
cover progress against the 30-day objectives, any blockers, and confirmation of
the probation outcome.

After the 30-day period the buddy arrangement ends and the line manager becomes
the single point of contact for support and escalation.


6. INTRODUCTORY SESSIONS

The following sessions are booked by the hiring manager and are expected to be
attended in the first three weeks:

  - Company overview and organisational structure (60 minutes)
  - Information security in practice (45 minutes)
  - Engineering delivery model and release gates (90 minutes, engineering only)
  - Travel, expenses and the corporate card (30 minutes)
  - Benefits, leave and working arrangements (45 minutes)


7. PROBATION

The standard probation period is six months. The outcome is confirmed in the
week-twelve check-in and again two weeks before the period ends. A probation
extension requires department head approval and must be communicated in
writing at least four weeks before the original end date.
