NORTHWIND SYSTEMS GMBH
CORPORATE CREDIT CARD RULES

Document ID: FIN-CRD-003
Version: 5.0
Effective date: 12 January 2026
Owner: Finance Operations
Classification: Internal
Review cycle: Annual


1. PURPOSE

These rules govern the issue and use of the Northwind corporate credit card.
They sit alongside the business travel and expense reimbursement policy, which
determines what is and is not a claimable expense. This document determines how
the card itself may be used.

Where the two documents differ on whether a cost is allowable, the travel and
expense policy takes precedence.


2. ELIGIBILITY AND ISSUE

A corporate card is issued to employees who travel on company business at least
four times a year, to employees who hold a delegated purchasing authority, and
to team leads who host customers or candidates.

Applications are submitted through Finance Operations and require line manager
approval. The card is issued in the employee's name, is not transferable, and
remains the property of Northwind Systems.

The standard monthly limit is EUR 5000. A higher limit requires department head
approval and is reviewed annually.


3. PERMITTED USE

The card is for business expenditure only. Permitted categories are:

  - Transport and accommodation booked for approved business travel.
  - Meals during business travel, within the limits in the travel policy.
  - Customer, candidate and supplier hospitality.
  - Conference and event registration fees that have been approved under the
    learning and development policy.
  - Small operational purchases within a delegated purchasing authority.

4. PROHIBITED USE

The card must not be used for:

  - Cash advances or cash withdrawals of any kind.
  - Personal purchases, including purchases the holder intends to repay.
  - Any expenditure for the personal benefit of the cardholder or their family.
  - Political donations.
  - Purchases that circumvent the procurement process, including splitting a
    single purchase into several transactions to stay under an approval limit.

Repeated prohibited use leads to withdrawal of the card and may be treated as a
disciplinary matter.


5. RECEIPTS

An itemised receipt is required for every transaction, without exception and
regardless of value. A card statement line is not a receipt: it shows the total
but not what was bought, and Finance cannot substantiate it for tax purposes.

Where a merchant issues only a summary total, the cardholder must obtain an
itemised version or attach a written explanation of what was purchased and for
whom.

Receipts are attached to the matching transaction in the expense tool. The
tool imports card transactions automatically within two business days of
settlement; the cardholder's task is to match, categorise and substantiate
each one.

Transactions with no receipt attached after sixty days are recovered through
payroll.


6. ACCIDENTAL PERSONAL USE

Occasional accidental personal use -- most often the wrong card taken out of a
wallet -- is not treated as misconduct if it is declared promptly.

The cardholder must flag the transaction in the expense tool as personal and
repay the amount to the company within fourteen days of the transaction date.
Repayment is made by bank transfer to the account shown on the expense tool's
repayment page, quoting the transaction reference.

Amounts not repaid within fourteen days are deducted from the next available
salary payment.


7. AIRPORT AND GROUND TRANSPORT

Ground transport taken during approved business travel may be charged to the
card. The pre-approval threshold in the travel and expense policy still
applies: a single journey above EUR 80 needs manager approval in advance, and
paying by card does not waive that requirement.


8. LOSS, THEFT AND DISPUTED TRANSACTIONS

Report a lost or stolen card to the card issuer immediately using the number
printed on the statement, and to Finance Operations the same business day.

Disputed or unrecognised transactions must be raised with Finance Operations
within thirty days of the statement date, after which the issuer will no longer
accept a chargeback.


9. LEAVING THE COMPANY

The card is surrendered to Finance Operations on the last working day. All
outstanding transactions must be substantiated and any personal amounts repaid
before final clearance is issued.
